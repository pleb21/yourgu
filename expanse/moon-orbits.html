Orbit Deck

STEP 03 · MOON SYSTEMS
REV. SEP 2026

Fifteen of the system's most consequential moons, grouped by planet. Pick a planet to load its system — only one animates at a time. Orbital distances are square-root scaled per system so the innermost and outermost moons both stay visible on screen; orbital periods are log-compressed on one shared scale across every planet, so Phobos's 7-hour lap and Iapetus's 79-day lap both animate at a watchable speed — and a moon that laps faster here laps faster in reality too.

Ring size = orbital distance (sqrt-scaled, per system) Loop speed = orbital period (log-compressed, shared scale) badge = retrograde orbit
