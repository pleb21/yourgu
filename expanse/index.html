Where the show fits in

Several of these bodies are locations from The Expanse itself: Luna and Deimos (Mars's inner moon) both appear as settings, and Ganymede — Jupiter's largest moon — is the "breadbasket of the outer planets" in the show. Two more major settings, Ceres and Eros, aren't moons at all — they're asteroids in the Belt, so they don't get console cards here, but they're candidates for a Belt-focused map later.

A couple of those show-location details are from memory rather than a re-check of the source — worth a second look before we treat them as settled if it matters for a future step.
